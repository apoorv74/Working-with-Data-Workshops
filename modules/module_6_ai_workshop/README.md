# Module 6: AI Workshop

## GIZ | FAIR FORWARD | AI Workshop | March 2024

### Overview
This workshop explores the role of Artificial Intelligence in Sustainable Rural Development. The presentation covers AI's evolution, components, and practical applications with a focus on strengthening data-driven governance.

### Presentation Details
- **Date**: March 21, 2024
- **Focus**: AI and its societal impact in rural development
- **Original URL**: https://bit.ly/ai-workshop-patna

### Key Topics Covered
1. AI's technological journey and historical context
2. Components of AI systems
3. Real-world applications in agriculture and governance
4. AI's potential and challenges in societal impact

### Notable Case Studies
- **Tumaini App**: Agricultural AI application
- **Wadhwani AI**: Cotton disease detection project
- **Jugalbandi for Government Schemes**: AI-powered governance solutions
